# Playlog brand guidelines

## Positioning

Playlog is a personal game journal: a calm, trustworthy place to track games, rate experiences, and remember a gaming history over time.

**Primary message:** Track. Rate. Remember.

## Identity idea

The mark combines three ideas: the letter P for Playlog, a bookmark for logging and remembering, and four controller buttons for gaming. The silhouette must remain readable at small sizes; the controller dots are supporting detail, not the primary shape.

## Visual language

- Deep black and charcoal surfaces create the focused base.
- Play green is the primary identity and action color.
- Teal is the focus indicator so keyboard focus remains distinct from green actions.
- Blue and violet are supporting accents, not competing primary colors.
- The full green-to-violet gradient is reserved for rare marketing emphasis.
- Interfaces favor generous spacing, clear hierarchy, restrained borders, and minimal decoration over glass effects or visual noise.

## Typography

Sora is the primary family. Use strong, compact headings and readable body copy. Avoid tiny metadata, excessively wide letter spacing, or using more than three weights in one view.

## Voice

- Premium: thoughtful and polished, never luxurious for its own sake.
- Minimal: direct and focused, never empty or vague.
- Trustworthy: precise about user data and system state, never manipulative.
- Passionate: written by people who care about games, never forced gamer slang.

## Product behavior

- Player first: user intent and control outrank engagement tricks.
- Meaningful tracking: context and memories matter more than vanity totals.
- Community: sharing is optional, respectful, and privacy-aware.
- Quality: accessible, responsive behavior is part of the brand, not final polish.

## Agent rules

- Read `tokens/colors.json` and `tokens/typography.json` instead of sampling images.
- Use logo files from `logos/`; do not redraw or generate substitutes.
- Use boards for composition and mood reference, not for extracting production pixels.
- Treat `docs/screenshots/` as implementation evidence only.
- Brand-in-use mobile artwork is a concept reference and does not authorize mobile work in this repository.
